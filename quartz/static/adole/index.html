Adole

Open source
Adole is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Ado, Universal Music, Cloud Nine or any other official party. No songs are available to download here. Please support Ado through her official releases.

Log in to play

Create a username + PIN (or log in with an existing one) to play Adole and keep your streak.

How to play

Each day has one Ado song. Play the clip, then guess the title.

  • You get 6 rounds. Clips grow: 1s, 2s, 3s, 4s, 6s, 10s.
  • Skip to unlock the next, longer clip.
  • Japanese, romaji and English titles all count.
  • Green is correct, orange shares a word, grey is wrong, black is skipped.
  • Hard mode: each clip plays once per round.
