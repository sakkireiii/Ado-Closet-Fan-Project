Think you know Ado's discography? Put your knowledge to the test!

Adole is an Ado-themed song-guessing game where you try to identify her songs from the clues provided. Whether you recognise a song within seconds or need a few hints to figure it out, the challenge is to see just how well you know Ado's music.

From familiar favourites to songs that might catch you off guard, every round is an opportunity to test your ears and revisit her discography.

## What's the challenge?

- **Guess the Song**: Identify the Ado song based on the audio or clues presented in the game.
- **Test Your Knowledge**: See how familiar you are with Ado's songs.
- **Challenge Yourself**: Recognize songs you might not listen to as often.

## Under Development

Adole is still under development. More features and improvements are planned, so stay tuned!
