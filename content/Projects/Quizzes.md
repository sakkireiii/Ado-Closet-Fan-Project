How well do you really know Ado?

Put your knowledge to the test with quizzes covering Ado, her music, discography, performances, and more.

Whether you're a longtime Adomin or someone who's still discovering her music, there's always something new to learn. Some questions might be easy, while others could put even the most dedicated fans to the test!

## What to Expect

- **Music & Discography**: Questions about Ado's songs, albums, covers, and releases.
- **Ado Trivia**: Test your knowledge of Ado and her musical journey.
- **Fan Challenges**: See how much you remember about different aspects of Ado's activities and works.

## Under Development

The quizzes are still under development. More topics, questions, and features will be added over time!

*This is an unofficial, fan-made project created by a passionate Adomin, for Adomins. Not affiliated with Ado or her official staff/team.*
