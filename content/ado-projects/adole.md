## How it works

Every day there's one Ado song. You get six rounds, and the clip gets longer each time: 1s, 2s, 3s, 4s, 6s, then 10s. Type a title, or skip to hear more. Japanese, Romaji and English titles all count.

- ✅ correct
- 🟧 shares a word with the answer
- ⬜ wrong
- ⬛ skipped

## Daily reset

A new song goes live every day at **12:00 AM Eastern Time** (Toronto time). Your streak follows the same clock, so play before it resets.

| Timezone | Reset time |
| --- | --- |
| Eastern Time (Toronto) | 12:00 AM |
| UTC | 5:00 AM (4:00 AM during daylight saving) |

Not sure where that lands for you? Search "12:00AM Toronto time" in your own timezone.

## What's inside

- A daily song, the same for every Adomin
- Hard mode: each clip plays once per round
- Username + PIN to keep your streak, plus a leaderboard
- An archive of previous songs
- A share button for your result
- Light and dark mode

## About

Adole² is inspired by the original [Adole](https://guess.adobnb.uk). Same daily-guess spirit, built separately for Ado Closet.

*This is an unofficial, fan-made project created by a passionate Adomin, for Adomins. Not affiliated with Ado or her official staff/team.*
