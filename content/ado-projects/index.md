A collection of fan-made projects and interactive experiences centered around Ado.

## Available Projects

- [Ado Ranker](https://ado-closet-fan-project.pages.dev/ado-projects/Ado-Ranker.md): Rank Ado's songs and create your own personal tier list.
- [Adole](adole): An Ado song-guessing game. How well do you know her discography?
- [Quizzes](Quizzes): Test your knowledge of Ado, her music, and related trivia.
