Can't decide how to rank Ado's songs? You're not alone.

Ado has so many incredible songs that choosing favourites, comparing them, and putting everything into a tier list can be a bit difficult. Sometimes you love two songs equally, keep changing your mind, or simply can't decide which one deserves the higher spot.

That's exactly why Ado Ranker exists!

Made for indecisive Adomins like me who struggle to rank Ado's songs, this page helps you work through those difficult choices, one comparison at a time. Instead of having to rank her entire discography all at once, you can compare songs against each other and gradually build a tier list that actually reflects your preferences.

## Features

- **Song Comparisons**: Choose between two songs and gradually establish your personal rankings.
- **Multiple Ranking Modes**: Try different comparison methods depending on how you want to rank.
- **Tie, Skip & Undo**: Can't decide? You can tie songs, skip a comparison, or undo your previous choice.
- **Difficulty Resolution**: Get help sorting out those particularly difficult comparisons through a series of questions about your preferences.
- **Manual Tier Assignment**: Adjust your rankings and place songs wherever you think they belong.
- **Search & Browse**: Easily find songs in your growing tier list.
- **Export & Backup**: Save your rankings and restore them whenever you need to.

---

There is no right or wrong way to rank Ado's songs. The goal isn't to produce an objective ranking, but to help you figure out your own preferences, even when you're having a hard time choosing.
